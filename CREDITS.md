# Credits

/usr/games Reborn is made by **Agun Wijaya**. Our code is released under the MIT licence
([`LICENSES/MIT.txt`](LICENSES/MIT.txt)); original notices for anything derived from the BSD games
are kept in [`LICENSES/`](LICENSES/README.md).

Everything below is taken from the files of the bsd-games distribution we worked from (`README`,
`AUTHORS`, `COPYING`, `hack/COPYRIGHT`, `phantasia/COPYRIGHT` and the manual pages). Where a source
says an author is unknown, so do we.

## The originals

The games of `/usr/games` were written by many people for BSD Unix from the late 1970s on. We worked
from **bsd-games**, the Linux package of the games in NetBSD that are free to modify and share. The
package was created by **Curt Olson** and **Andy Tefft**; **Joseph S. Myers** took over its
maintenance in 1997 and fixed many bugs along the way. The games' own copyrights belong mostly to
**The Regents of the University of California**, with others held by **The NetBSD Foundation**,
**Stichting Centrum voor Wiskunde en Informatica** (Amsterdam), individual authors, and one game
(`phantasia`) that was deliberately left uncopyrighted.
