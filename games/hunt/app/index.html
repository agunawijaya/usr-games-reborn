CONTROLS

      
Modern keys can be remapped: click a key, then press the new one (Esc cancels). Classic keys are hunt's own and stay as they are. Every action is still one hunt keystroke.

      

      

    


    

      
HOW TO PLAY

      
Kill the others before they kill you. Your score is kills per entry. You move without turning — facing is separate, and you only fire the way you face. You only see ahead and to your sides, never behind; what you saw stays on your map as a ghost. (Set Fog to Off on the setup or the pause menu and the whole maze is lit; the match still counts.) Firing shows every player where you are. Shots move five cells per step, bounce 90° off mirrors — and every hit flips the mirror. Any blast destroys interior walls; they grow back, sometimes as mirrors or doors. Doors scatter shots.

      
In the career, and in a free match with a goal, you win by tagging the goal's number of rivals before you are hit out three times. Settings can make it gentler: Fog Off lights the whole maze, Bot speed slows the bots, Turn by turn lets the world move only when you press a key (. waits a step), and the Easy and Aim controls drop the separate facing keys (under Aim, Shift with an arrow fires that way and Alt with an arrow throws a grenade).
