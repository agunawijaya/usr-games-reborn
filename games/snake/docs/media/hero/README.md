- **a.** Chamber 4 of a run: a satchel bulging with glints, the snake at 60 % boldness (its crown
  glowing, its pupils narrowed) and the strike preview showing where it may land next.
- **b.** Caught: the snake coils round you with a smug look, the satchel bursts and the glints
  scatter; the Lucky Break dial spins against your pockets' last digit.
- **c.** At the door: the satchel poured into the vault, the counter and a best-haul ribbon, and the
  snake sulking in the far corner.
- **Bonus.** Caught on a record run, the snake winks before the dial spins.

The "Pause · Esc" button at the top right stands in for the Hall's own, which the player draws over
every native game.
