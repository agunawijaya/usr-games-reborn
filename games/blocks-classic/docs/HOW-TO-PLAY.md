# How to play Broken Well

## Goal

Clear a shift's line target before the stack reaches the top of the well, or, on a flood shift,
hold the well together through its target number of rising rubble rows. A career shift also comes
with three contracts; meeting them earns seals but is never required to clear the shift.

## Controls

| Action              | Keyboard               |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Move left / right     | ← / → or A / D          |
| Rotate clockwise       | ↑ or W or X              |
| Rotate counter-clockwise | Z                       |
| Soft drop              | ↓ or S                   |
| Hard drop               | Space                    |
| Pause                   | Esc or P                 |

There is no mouse or drag control for falling pieces — pieces move by discrete steps, which the
keyboard already does precisely. The pause button in the top bar is the mouse alternative to Esc.

## The well

Each shift gives you one of eight shapes: the Open Shaft (a plain rectangle), the Canyon (a narrow
cut), the Split Shaft (a central pillar), the Hourglass Neck, the Donut Pillar, the Staircase, the
Tower (tall and narrow) and the Wide Cut (broad and shallow). Rock cells are permanent — a line
only clears across the cells that are not rock.

## Modes

- **The Long Dig** (marathon): clear the shift's line target. Some shifts start you on a rubble
  stack left by the last crew.
- **The Rising Flood** (survival): no starting rubble, but a rubble row rises from below at a
  shortening interval as your level climbs. Hold the well through the shift's target number of
  rows.

## Career, contracts and rank

Twelve shifts, each opening the next once the one before is cleared. Every shift's briefing lists
three contracts — for example "finish in 70 pieces or fewer" or "clear a Tetris: four lines at
once." Your rank (Hand Digger through Master of the Broken Well) follows how many shifts you have
cleared, shown on the game menu.

## Free Dig and the Daily Shift

Free Dig lets you choose the well shape, starting rubble and mode yourself, with no contracts and
no effect on your career. The Daily Shift gives everyone the same well on the same day, with three
contracts of its own; finishing it once a day is all it asks, and its report gives you a one-line
summary to share, with no link.

## Scoring

A hard drop and a soft drop both add a small bonus per cell dropped. Clearing lines scores more the
more you clear at once (a Tetris — four lines in one drop — pays the most) and scales with your
current level, which rises as you clear lines.

## The logbook

Every finished shift is written up in the Logbook, reachable from the game menu: the well you
played, what you cleared, and how it ended.
