Principal phases

    Every little Moon here is rendered by the same shader as the big one, from pom’s own numbers.

    Selene

    The phase of the Moon, computed by pom, the BSD program Keith E. Brandt wrote in 1984 from Peter Duffett-Smith’s Practical Astronomy with Your Calculator. This port reproduces its output byte for byte, then paints the answer.

    No images were used. The Moon, its seas and craters, the stars, the Milky Way, the hills and the lake are all computed by shaders at load time. The seas and named craters sit at their real coordinates; the lit fraction and the terminator come straight from pom’s potm().
