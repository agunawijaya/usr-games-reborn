        BATTLESTARPajamas to Paradise

        Riggle's stellar-tropical adventure (Berkeley, 1979–84), rebuilt from its original source. You wake in silk pajamas aboard a dying battlestar. Everything you see and hear is generated by code.

        The original checked your Unix login against Berkeley's hereditary wizards. Type one of their names here to begin as a wizard, or leave it empty.

        Saved games

          Type save in the game (as in 1984) to make a named save. Loading one replays the start of a turn, exactly like battlestar -r.

          How to play

          Moving

          You only ever move relative to where you face: ahead back left right up down (or a b l r u d). There is no north or south — a compass, if you find one, tells you where north is. Click the arrows on the scene to move too.

          Doing

          take/get, drop, wear, take off, draw, put on, i (inventory), look, eat, drink, light, use, give … to …, kiss, kill, shoot, throw, open, dig, bury, ride, drive, jump, follow, sleep, launch, land, time, score, save, verbose/brief, quit. Chain with commas or and: take knife, laser.

          Keys

          Tab completes a word · ↑/↓ history · ` hints · Shift+` Override · Ctrl+M world map · F1 help · Esc closes panels.

          Dogfights

          Your keys don't move a cursor: they set how the raider drifts across your sights (you are turning the ship). Arrows or h l j k (r l u d in the original), Shift for a hard turn. F/Space fires two torpedoes; they hit only when the raider sits on the centre line. All dogfights share one 120-second clock.

          Scoring

          Three axes — PLEASURE, POWER, EGO — and a title from the strongest (Don Juan, Darth Vader, Mr. Roarke…). Winning is its own story.
