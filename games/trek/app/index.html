TREK — How to Play

Deep Space · Procedural · BSD trek(6) spiritual successor

The Mission

You are captain of the USS Enterprise. Klingon warships have infiltrated Federation space across an 8×8 galaxy of quadrants. Hunt down and destroy every Klingon before the stardate budget runs out — or the Federation falls.

Everything you see and hear in this version — nebulae, ships, the starbase, stars, beams, explosions, the chart, the sound — is generated by code at runtime. No images, no audio files.

The Screen

Your ship is the long pale cruiser with a glowing cyan sensor band and three blue engines. When shields are up, a translucent bubble surrounds it; hits light up a ring of hexagons where they land.

Klingon ships are dark bronze (or jade) hulls with red or teal engine glow; each turns its bow to face you. The starbase is the huge ring station with lit windows — dock next to it. Stars are navigation obstacles that swallow torpedoes and light up nearby hulls.

The HUD

Ship Status (top-left): shields / hull / energy / torpedoes. Systems (top-right): OK / WEAK / DAMAGED. Sector Contacts (right): what is in this quadrant. Bridge Log (bottom-right): recent events.

Commands

Type each command at the Command > prompt and press Enter.

phaser 500
Fire phasers with 500 energy at all Klingons here. Damage split, weaker with distance.
torpedo 3
Photon torpedo at bearing 3 (north). 50 energy + 1 torpedo. Hits the first thing on its path.
move 3 2
Course 3, warp 2. Energy ≈ warp²×10, plus stardates. Warp < 1 = impulse inside the quadrant.
impulse 3
Short impulse move on bearing 3.
srscan / lrscan
Short-range / long-range scan (lrscan lifts the fog on adjacent quadrants).
damages
Subsystem damage report.
dock
Dock at an adjacent starbase — full refuel, repair, rearm.
shields up / down / 300
Raise / lower shields, or transfer energy into them.
help · quit
Inline command list · end the mission.

Bearing — Clock Face

Views

Tactical: the current quadrant. Galaxy Chart: an 8×8 hologram with fog of war. Toggle with V (only when the command line is empty) or the bezel buttons.

Winning & Losing

  • ✓ Destroy every Klingon before the stardate budget expires.
  • ✗ Stardate budget runs out.
  • ✗ Hull destroyed.
  • ✗ Life support fails.
  • ✗ Energy exhausted.

Captain's Override

! (or typing override) opens the Captain's Override: reveal the galaxy map, infinite energy, infinite torpedoes, invulnerable shields, freeze the stardate clock, one-shot kills, instant warp by clicking the chart, and a full repair/resupply. Using any of them marks the mission CHEATED.

override
Open / close the panel.
override energy on
Flags: map energy torps shields clock oneshot warp — add on/off, or omit to toggle.
override warp 3-5
Engage instant warp and jump to quadrant 3-5.
override resupply
Full repair and resupply, anywhere.
override off
Release every override (the mission stays marked).
