DELVERS' LORE & SURVIVAL GUIDE

The Objective: Deep within these Dwarven halls slumbers the dreaded Wumpus. Track its lair through sensory cues and loose a crooked arrow into its chamber before you stumble into its jaws or fall prey to the abyss.

Sensory Cues:

  • Cold Draft (💨): A bottomless pit lies directly adjacent (1 hop). Stepping into a pit is fatal, though a skilled wanderer has a 2-in-12 chance of grabbing a rock outcrop.
  • Stench (👃): The foul odor of the beast drifts across up to 2 chambers. Take heed when loosed arrows miss!
  • Bat Flutter (🦇): Super-bats roost in an adjacent room. They will seize you and deposit you in a random cave chamber!

Crooked Arrows: Your magic arrows can bend through up to 5 chambers. Be wary: ricochets that return to your room will strike you down!
